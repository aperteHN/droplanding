Airdrop Q&A.

What does it cost to join, and are Sybil checks applied?

There is no entry price. Each application only requires a small network gas fee. Separate Sybil detection criteria do not apply because every entry requires payment of that gas fee.

How do I participate?

Log in to your RWAI account, connect your wallet, select Buy Now, choose Base or BSC, approve the transaction, and check the result in My Page.

How often can I apply?

One entry per day per account.

How are rewards allocated, and does platform activity matter?

Rewards scale proportionally with the number of participants. Diverse activity across the RWAI platform will also be considered for the airdrop.

Where can I check distributed assets?

Distributed assets will be added to your participant account and can be viewed on the RWAI platform.

When does the event end?

The event runs until further notice and may end early if the number of participating wallets becomes excessively high. When it ends, updates will be announced on the RWAI X account and this page.

What do entries represent?

Entries are participation records, not assets for ownership or trading.
